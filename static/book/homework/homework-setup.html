2  Homework: Setting Up

Download this assignment — unzip it, open homework-setup.qmd in Positron, and answer inside the answer blocks.

What this assignment is

Your setup. There is one exercise and it has nothing to do with statistics: install the software, get a photo of something in your room into the answer block below, and hand it in. The point is to find out now, while nothing is riding on it, that the whole path works—because every assignment after this one runs on the same path, and some of them will ask you to photograph work you did on paper.

Set up once

  1. Download and install Positron.

  2. In Positron, open Extensions in the sidebar, search for tlda Classroom, and choose Install. If you already installed it, choose Update instead.

  3. Open the class app and register. Choose Connect Positron, allow the page to open Positron, then choose Continue to class. You only do this once.

Optional: turn on visual mode

Click the … at the right of the editor toolbar and choose Edit in Visual Mode. ⇧⌘F4 does the same thing, and switches you back.

Visual mode shows the document roughly as it will come out, so you can see the answer blocks you are writing in. The next step works with or without visual mode.

Putting a photo in an answer block

Copy the photo, then right-click—Command-click if you have a Mac—where you want it inside the answer block, and choose Paste.

Positron copies the photo into the same folder as this document, and that copy is what makes it travel with your work when you hand it in. If you would rather do it by hand, put the file in that folder yourself and write ![](my-photo.png) with your photo’s filename.

Exercise

Exercise 4.1  

Exercise

Take a photo of something in the room you are in, and include it here.

Anything will do — a plant, a mug, the wall. What matters is that the photo ends up inside your submitted file, because that is the part that breaks if something is set up wrong.

SolutionSolution

Any photo of anything, pasted into the answer block.

Handing it in

  1. Preview it. Click Preview and make sure your homework looks like you intended it to.
  2. Submit. Open View > Command Palette (or press Shift-Command-P on a Mac), type Homework: Submit, and choose it. At the moment, this only works outside visual mode, so press Shift-Command-F4 to switch back first if necessary; we are working on fixing that.

If it reports a problem you don’t know how to fix, talk to me after class or email me at davidahirshberg@emory.edu.
